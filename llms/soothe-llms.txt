# Soothe

> Soothe is a Los Angeles-based on-demand wellness marketplace, founded in 2013, that
> connects consumers and businesses with independent, licensed wellness professionals
> who deliver massage, facial and skincare, and hair and beauty services at the
> customer's own location. It operates in 40+ US cities and in Australia, Canada and
> the United Kingdom, and runs a B2B practice covering corporate wellness, hospitality
> and spa staffing, residential communities and event chair massage.

Important for agents: Soothe publishes NO public developer program and NO public
booking API. Its API host, https://api.soothe.com, redirects every request to a
credentialed sign-in form. The corporate and hospitality "booking platform" Soothe
markets is reached through a contact-sales form, not through documentation. Do not
attempt to construct calls against api.soothe.com; there is no published contract for
it. Bookings are made by humans through the Soothe app or the web booking flow.

## APIs

- [Soothe Help Center Mirror](https://help.soothe.com/): The only machine-readable
  contract Soothe serves. A FastAPI application that serves a self-hosted mirror of the
  company's Document360 help centre — page serving, an upstream proxy, a mirror manifest
  and a health check. Anonymous and read-only. This is NOT the booking API.

## Specs

- [OpenAPI 3.1.0 — Help Center Mirror](https://help.soothe.com/openapi.json): 4 paths,
  6 operations, no securitySchemes, no servers block.
- [Mirror manifest](https://help.soothe.com/manifest.json): the crawlable index of all
  541 mirrored help-centre page paths, with the mirror timestamp.
- [Mirror health](https://help.soothe.com/health): status, page count, last mirror time.
